## Research interests

I study how to infer interaction laws and governing dynamics from observations of biological and physical systems. I also develop mathematically interpretable representations of these systems using geometric algebra and geometric machine learning.

## Selected research experience

### Graduate Researcher, MIT

*September 2024–present · Advisor: Jörn Dunkel*

- Investigating methods to infer interaction laws and governing dynamics of interacting systems, including animal behavior.
- Developing interpretable representations of biological and physical systems using geometric algebra.
- SM thesis: Developed a method to learn operator algebras from experimental data by constructing the ideal of relations from the null space of an experimental data matrix.

### Post-baccalaureate Research Assistant, Yale

*August 2023–August 2024 · Krishnaswamy Lab; advisor: Smita Krishnaswamy*

- Developed geometric deep learning methods for graph signals and high-dimensional biological data, including hypergraph wavelets for spatial transcriptomics.
- Contributed to the mathematical development of DYMAG and implemented chaotic dynamics for graph message passing.

### Sweeney Lab, Yale

*September 2021–May 2023 · Advisor: Alison Sweeney*

- Used Monte Carlo simulations in C to study semiflexible polymers on cylindrical surfaces under confinement.
- Developed image-processing algorithms to characterize polymer network topology in cryoEM images.
