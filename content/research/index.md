I develop computational methods to discover the physical and mathematical structure underlying living systems. My research brings together geometric algebra, machine learning, and dynamics identification.

## Selected research

### Structure and dynamics in living systems

At MIT, I work with [Jörn Dunkel](https://math.mit.edu/~dunkel/) on computational approaches to uncovering structure in living systems, drawing on geometric algebra and dynamics identification.

### Graph representation learning for biological data

Previously, in [Smita Krishnaswamy's lab](https://krishnaswamylab.org) at Yale, I developed graph representation learning methods to study high-dimensional biological data.
